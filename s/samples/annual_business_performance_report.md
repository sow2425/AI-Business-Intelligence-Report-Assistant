# FY2026 Annual Business & Regional Performance Report

## Executive Summary
Fiscal Year 2026 marked record enterprise adoption across all core software lines, driven by strong multi-year renewals in Cloud Suite and rapid expansion of AI Analytics. Overall gross margin improved by 3.4 percentage points compared to the prior year due to infrastructure optimization and direct enterprise deal sizing.

## Regional Performance Highlights

### North America
North America delivered the strongest overall business performance in FY2026, leading every major financial KPI.
North America achieved a 94% enterprise customer retention rate and shortened average enterprise sales cycles from 74 days to 51 days after launching dedicated solutions engineering pods.
Strong adoption of bundled Cloud Suite + AI Analytics enterprise contracts in North America increased average revenue per account (ARPA) by 29% YoY.
Direct Enterprise account expansion in financial services and healthcare accounted for over 68% of North America's net-new annual recurring revenue.

### Europe (EMEA)
Europe maintained steady double-digit growth anchored by Security Ops compliance demand following new EU data residency regulations.
However, longer procurement cycles and heavy reliance on third-party channel partners compressed regional operating margins relative to North America.

### APAC (Asia-Pacific)
APAC emerged as the fastest-accelerating emerging territory in H2 after onboarding 18 regional systems integrators and launching localized billing.
Self-Serve Web conversions grew strongly in Q3-Q4, though average deal size remains smaller than North America.

### Latin America
Latin America experienced modest single-digit growth due to currency headwinds, higher partner onboarding costs, and elevated price sensitivity in SMB accounts.

## Operational Risks and Challenges
Partner-led deals in Europe and Latin America experienced 14% higher discounting than Direct Enterprise contracts, reducing net profit margin.
Support ticket volume rose 19% in Q4 for legacy Security Ops deployments, creating fulfillment bottlenecks during peak renewals.

## Strategic Priorities for Next Fiscal Year
Expand North America's solutions-engineering bundle playbook into APAC and Europe enterprise accounts.
Introduce automated onboarding templates for Security Ops to reduce support overhead and protect profit margins in trailing regions.
